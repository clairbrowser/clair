## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-23

- New: Clair has its own window: tabs, the address and the browser controls are in a sidebar on the left, with the page in a card beside it. Cmd+T and Cmd+L (File > Open Location Bar) open a command bar for searching, typing an address or switching to an open tab, and pressing them again closes it; new tabs go at the top of the list, and View > Hide Sidebar (Cmd+S) hides the sidebar, which shows again when you move the pointer to the window's left edge
- New: Drag from the top of a page to move the window, as in Arc: a drag that starts in the page's top 59 points moves the window unless the pointer is over something the page uses, such as text or a link. Turn it off in Settings > Advanced
- New: Right-click the back or forward button to see the tab's history in that direction, with each page's icon, and choose a page to go there (Cmd-click opens it in a new background tab). A loading page shows Arc's small pulsing capsule above the page. The reload button no longer flickers to stop while a loaded page's frames or ads keep loading, and it holds still for a moment if a load ends under the pointer, so a late click doesn't reload. With every window closed, the Dock menu offers New Incognito Window
- New: Settings… (Cmd+,) opens Clair's own Settings window instead of a Chromium settings tab. General shows whether Clair is your default browser and can make it so. Advanced has the switch for dragging the window from the top of webpages, and More Settings… for search, privacy and Chromium's other settings
- New: Extensions install and update from the Chrome Web Store, so Clair now contacts Google for store extensions (their IDs and versions are sent with update checks). Clair asks before adding or removing one in a native sheet, pins new extensions to the sidebar, shows popups in their own small window, adds right-click menus to extensions, doesn't open their uninstall pages, and supports chrome.action.openPopup()
- New: The command bar can delete suggestions from history, as in Arc. Hover over a page you've visited or an earlier search and click its ×, or press ⌥⌘⌫ to delete the selected one
- New: Shift+click a link to open it in a Peek over the current page, as in Arc. Close it with its × button, Esc, ⌘W or a click beside it, or expand it into a tab right below the one it came from. The new Links page in Settings turns this off, so Shift+click opens a new window again
- New: Spaces, as in Arc: keep work, personal and project tabs apart, each Space with its own name and emoji. The sidebar shows the current Space above its tabs, and a switcher in its footer moves between Spaces with a sideways slide; Spaces › Next Space (Opt+Cmd+Right), Previous Space (Opt+Cmd+Left) and Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 do too. Create a Space from the footer's plus menu or the Spaces menu, rename it, change its icon or delete it from its header's menu, drag it in the switcher to reorder, or manage every Space at once with Manage Spaces…. A tab's menu moves it to another Space with its page as it was. Closing a tab goes back to the tab you came from, as in Arc, and Next and Previous Tab are now Opt+Cmd+Down and Up. Clair also reopens your tabs at launch, each in its Space; "On startup" in Settings still changes that
- New: Profiles: each Space uses a profile with its own cookies, logins, history and extensions. Choose it when creating a Space or in Manage Spaces, change it with Set Profile or Spaces › Change Profile, and add, rename or remove profiles in Settings › Profiles, where each profile also has its download location and links to its search, privacy, notification and browsing data settings. Switching to a Space of another profile stays in the same window
- New: Pinned tabs: each Space keeps the sites you return to above its Today tabs. Pin a tab with Cmd+D or by dragging it, and its page can close while the pin stays; the − button and Cmd+W close the page, × removes the pin, and Reopen Closed Tab brings a removed pin back. A pinned tab you navigated away from shows its pinned page's icon, which takes you back, and Edit Pinned Page changes its URL. The chevron by the Space's name collapses the section, and Clear on the divider, or Shift+Cmd+K, closes the Today tabs
- New: Favorites: a grid of tiles at the top of the sidebar for each profile's most used sites, the same in every Space of the profile. Add one with Pin to Favorites or by dragging a tab onto the grid
- New: Rename tabs: double-click a tab or choose Rename… to give it a name of its own, which stays through navigation and relaunches and is also the window's title. Favorites rename in a dialog
- New: Links to other sites from a pinned tab or favorite open in a Peek, so the pinned page stays as it was. Settings › Links can turn it off
- New: Link rules, in Settings › Links, open links from other apps in a Space you choose: URLs containing a text, one exact URL, or URLs starting with a text
- New: Clair now turns off store extensions that Google has pulled as malware, for breaking the store's policy or as unwanted, as Chrome does, and a dialog names any found to contain malware. Store update checks now go to Google's update service at update.googleapis.com, and send no more than before
- New: Clair now checks certificates against Chromium's list of revoked certificates, as Chrome does, so a site whose certificate has been revoked shows a certificate error. Clair fetches the list from Google's update service at update.googleapis.com, over HTTPS, about a minute after launch and every five hours, and downloads it when it changes. Each check sends only the list's version, the browser version and the platform, with no language or identifiers
- New: A waiting update shows "New Clair Version Available" at the bottom of the sidebar, with Restart and Update on hover, and the first launch after an update says "Update Complete!" with a link to what's new
- New: Back, forward, reload, the address and pinned extensions now sit in a toolbar above the page, as Arc's "Show as Toolbar" does, on by default. View › Hide Toolbar (Shift+Cmd+D), the toolbar's menu and a new "Location bar" choice on Settings › General move them back into the sidebar
- New: Bookmarks are back as one list in a Bookmarks menu: Bookmark This Page… (⇧⌘B) adds the page and opens a dialog to name it, file it in a folder or remove it, and reads Edit Bookmark… on a bookmarked page; Manage Bookmarks (⌥⌘B) opens the bookmark manager; and the command bar suggests bookmarks, with a bookmark icon. Clair has no bookmarks bar, so bookmarks saved or imported there move to the Bookmarks menu, and a one-time notice says so
- New: With no favorites, dragging a tab above the Space header shows where favorites go, as in Arc, and dropping it there makes it a favorite
- Changed: The disk image's window has a lighter look, with install instructions at the top and arrows from Clair to Applications
- Changed: Save Page As is now Shift+Cmd+S. Bookmarks, password saving and autofill are gone from the new window: export saved passwords from chrome://password-manager/settings before relying on another password manager. Tab groups, split view, side panels such as reading mode, tab search, Lens, translate, Cast and the EyeDropper aren't available in it
- Changed: The sidebar is closer to Arc: it slides in sooner and faster when you move the pointer to the left edge, Show Sidebar docks a revealed sidebar where it is, the page keeps its margins when the sidebar hides, and you can drag the window from the sidebar's empty space. Its buttons have tooltips with their shortcuts, the URL field's buttons and hover highlights fade, tabs have Arc's favicon and close button sizes, and the window dims its controls when it isn't in front. The link preview at the bottom of the page fades in and out, the command bar's field stays put as results appear, and incognito windows use a crossed-out eye
- Changed: New windows open with the command bar ready for typing. Edit > Copy URL copies the page's address with Shift+Cmd+C, as in Arc (Opt+Cmd+C still inspects an element), and the URL field's right-click menu offers it too. Every button has a tooltip with its shortcut, and resting on a tab for a few seconds shows its full title. The URL field hides "www.", shows its buttons whenever the pointer is over the sidebar, and matches Arc's look in Light and Dark Mode, as do the open tab in dark and incognito windows and the incognito badge. A hidden sidebar shows from anywhere along the window's left edge and is less see-through, and the sidebar's buttons sit in the right place in full screen
- Changed: Copying the page's address (Shift+Cmd+C, the URL field's copy button or its menu) shows Arc's "Copied Current URL" popup in the corner of the page, with a share button, and the full-screen "Press Esc" notice uses the same popup. Tooltips have key caps with depth, a softer shadow and tighter padding, a tab's full title shows after 2 seconds, and clicking or right-clicking a control no longer brings its tooltip up. A tab's speaker sits between its icon and title, as in Arc, and animates between playing and muted. The command bar's border matches Arc's, in full screen the sidebar no longer flickers darker when the pointer moves over it, and tooltips, popups, the URL field and the command bar match Arc in Dark Mode and incognito windows
- Changed: Every sidebar button now highlights under the pointer and dims when the window isn't in front, including pinned extensions and the downloads button. The URL field's buttons are smaller, as in Arc, and pinned extensions and the extensions button match the downloads button's size. The tab close button and the find bar's and infobar's buttons have tooltips and VoiceOver labels, and tooltip text is a little bolder
- Changed: The sidebar has a footer like Arc's. The downloads button is always there, where Arc has its library button, even before anything has been downloaded, and its progress matches the Dock's in every window. A plus button at the other end opens a menu with New Tab
- Changed: A sidebar revealed from the window's edge hides sooner once the pointer leaves it, as in Arc
- Changed: A hidden sidebar also slides in when the pointer overshoots the window's left edge, up to 250 points past it, or within 50 points of it from elsewhere, as in Arc
- Changed: Pinned extensions are now tiles that wrap across the sidebar, as in Arc, and the extensions button is gone. The site-controls button, now on every tab including new tabs, opens a native Site Control Center with every extension, pin badges, a tile for adding more, Site Info and a menu. Popups for extensions that aren't pinned open beside it. A new Extensions menu in the menu bar lists every extension and opens its popup the same way. A red lock shows in the URL field on pages that aren't secure, extensions that are off for the tab no longer run when clicked, and a revealed sidebar stays put while the Site Control Center or downloads list is open
- Changed: Extension install, remove and permission confirmations are now Clair's own dialogs, styled like Arc's: a card on the window with the extension's icon, and Esc and Return shown on the Cancel and default buttons. A long permission list scrolls inside the dialog
- Changed: A Space's tabs are now the same in every window, as in Arc: each window lists every tab of the Space it shows, and opening, closing or reordering a tab in one shows in the others at once. Selecting a tab another window shows brings its page here without reloading it, Peek included, and leaves that window a dimmed still of it until you click in it again. Closing a window keeps its tabs in your other window; closing the last one closes them, as before
- Changed: Closing a Space's last tab keeps the window on that Space, on a new tab page, as in Arc; closing that new tab page closes the window
- Changed: Move to Space follows the tab when it's the one you're on, and otherwise says where it went
- Changed: Settings opens on General each time
- Changed: Clair opens with one window on the Space you last used, as in Arc. Turn on "When opening Clair, restore windows from previous session" in Settings › Advanced to bring every window back
- Changed: "Warn before quitting" moved from the Clair menu to Settings › Advanced
- Changed: Cmd+D pins and unpins the tab, as in Arc, instead of doing nothing
- Changed: Tabs always come back when Clair opens, as in Arc: chrome://settings no longer has an On startup section, and a startup page set there or by an extension is ignored
- Changed: "Warn before quitting" asks in a dialog, as Dia does, instead of making you hold ⌘Q, and "Always quit" stops it asking
- Changed: Clair's links, About window and update checks point to its new home, github.com/clairbrowser/clair
- Changed: When an extension asks to remove another one, the remove dialog now names the extension that asked, so it no longer looks like a removal you started yourself
- Changed: The Extensions menu lists extensions by the names they were installed under, as chrome://extensions shows them, and the Site Control Center orders them by those names, whatever title an extension gives its button. The title stays the button's tooltip
- Changed: Clair now keeps Chromium's Certificate Transparency log list, key pins and trusted root certificates current, as Chrome does, so sites keep being checked against them as the browser ages, and certificate authorities that Chromium stops trusting stop being trusted without waiting for a Clair update. It fetches them in the same check as the revocation list, over HTTPS, and the check sends nothing more. Clair also keeps forcing HTTPS on the sites in its built-in HSTS preload list, however old the browser is
